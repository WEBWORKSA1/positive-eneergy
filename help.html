Need help now?

If you're struggling, you deserve real support from a real person. Positive.Energy is a self-help site, not a crisis service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now (911 in the US and Canada, 999 or 112 in the UK, 112 in the EU, 000 in Australia, 112 in India).

Free, confidential support lines

  • United States: call or text 988 (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
  • Canada: call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline).
  • United Kingdom & Ireland: Samaritans, 116 123, free, 24/7.
  • Australia: Lifeline, 13 11 14.
  • India: Tele-MANAS, 14416 or 1-800-891-4416.
  • Elsewhere: find a free helpline in your country at findahelpline.com.

When to see a professional

Positive practices help many people feel better, but they are not a treatment for depression, anxiety disorders, trauma or other health conditions. Please talk to a doctor or licensed therapist if low mood, worry or exhaustion lasts more than two weeks, gets in the way of work or relationships, or if you notice changes in sleep, appetite or interest in things you used to enjoy.

A note on "toxic positivity"

Positive energy doesn't mean pretending everything is fine. Difficult emotions are normal and useful signals. Our approach is to acknowledge them, look after yourself, and reach for support. Forcing a smile isn't part of it.
