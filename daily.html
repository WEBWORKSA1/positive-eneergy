Why a daily ritual works

Positive emotions don't build up like a savings account. They work more like exercise. Barbara Fredrickson's "broaden-and-build" research suggests that frequent small positive moments widen your attention and, over weeks, build lasting resources: resilience, social connection and creative problem-solving. The key word is frequent. Five minutes every day beats one big effort a month.

That's why this page changes every day. Make it your morning tab: read the quote, say the affirmation out loud, and pick a time today for the practice. Then tick it off in the 30-day challenge.
