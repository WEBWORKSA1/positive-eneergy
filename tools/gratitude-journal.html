The "three good things" exercise

Each evening, write down three things that went well and why. In Martin Seligman's research this was one of the most effective positive-psychology exercises tested, with benefits reported for months in some participants. The explanation step matters, so don't skip the "why".

Your entries are stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us. Read next: 7 gratitude practices that work.

FAQ

Is my journal private?

Yes. Entries are saved only in your browser's local storage on this device. We never receive them.

What should I write in a gratitude journal?

Specific moments, people and small wins, plus why they happened, for example 'my colleague covered my shift because I'd helped her last week'.

Tools are for general wellbeing and self-reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. Data you enter is stored only in your browser.
