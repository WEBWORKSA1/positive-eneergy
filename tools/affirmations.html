    How to use affirmations so they work

    Research suggests believable, value-based affirmations help more than grand claims. Pick one that feels true-ish, say it once in the morning, then do one small action that proves it. More in Do affirmations work?

    FAQ

    What is a good daily affirmation?

    A believable, specific statement tied to a value or action, such as 'I'm learning to speak up in meetings', paired with a small action.

    Can affirmations backfire?

    Broad statements that feel untrue can make some people feel worse, so choose realistic, process-focused wording.
