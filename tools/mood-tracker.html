Why track mood and energy?

Memory is a poor record of how we felt. Brief, regular check-ins reveal patterns: which habits, people and days lift you, and which drain you. Tag consistently and the tracker will tell you which tag lines up with your highest energy. Read: how to protect your energy.

FAQ

How often should I log my mood?

Once or twice a day at similar times works well. Consistency makes patterns visible.

Where is my mood data stored?

Only in your browser on this device. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Tools are for general wellbeing and self-reflection, not diagnosis or treatment. Data you enter is stored only in your browser.
