How the Energy Score works

The quiz rates 16 everyday behaviours across four energy sources: physical (sleep, movement, nutrition), emotional (feelings, connection, appreciation), mental (focus, self-talk, recovery) and purpose (meaning, flow, goals, contribution). Each answer scores 1–5, and the total is scaled to 0–100.

It's a self-reflection tool, not a clinical assessment. Use it as a snapshot. Retake it monthly to see what's changing.

FAQ

How accurate is the Energy Score?

It's a reflective self-assessment based on everyday habits, useful for spotting patterns but not a medical or psychological test.

How often should I take it?

Once a month is a good rhythm to track change after building new habits.
