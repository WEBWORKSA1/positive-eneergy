Why breathing changes how you feel

Breathing is one of the few parts of your stress response you can control directly. Slow breathing with a longer exhale is associated with a shift toward the body's "rest and digest" state, while faster breathing feels more activating. A 2023 Stanford study comparing breathwork and mindfulness found five minutes of daily cyclic sighing improved mood in participants.

FAQ

What is box breathing?

Inhaling, holding, exhaling and holding again for equal counts, typically four seconds each, used to steady attention under pressure.

Is breathwork safe?

Gentle breathing exercises are safe for most people. Stop if you feel dizzy, and check with a doctor if you have a respiratory or heart condition.
