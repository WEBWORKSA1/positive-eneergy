Why reframing works
Cognitive behavioural therapy is one of the most researched approaches in psychology, and identifying and challenging unhelpful thoughts is one of its core skills. Writing it down creates distance, and testing the evidence makes the thought more accurate rather than artificially cheerful. Read: How to think more positively.
FAQ
What is cognitive reframing?
A technique from cognitive behavioural therapy where you identify an unhelpful thought, examine the evidence and replace it with a more balanced one.
What are common thinking traps?
All-or-nothing thinking, catastrophising, mind reading, should statements, labelling, mental filtering, fortune telling and personalising.
