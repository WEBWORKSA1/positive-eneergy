Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Summary

  • Our wellbeing tools (journal, mood tracker, challenge, reframer, favourites) store data only in your browser. We never receive it.
  • When you submit a form, we receive what you type so we can respond.
  • Google AdSense uses cookies to serve and measure ads.

Information you give us

Forms (contact, coach matching, workplace inquiries, contests, applications, pledges and newsletter) collect the details you enter, such as name, email and message. Form submissions are delivered to us through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-processing service. We use this information only to respond, fulfil your request and, if you opt in, send our newsletter.

Coach matching

If you request coach matches and give consent, we share your request details with up to three independent coaches who may contact you. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our cookie banner lets you choose "Essential only", which requests non-personalised ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are shown a Google-certified consent message where required.

Embedded content

YouTube videos are embedded in privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you press play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. Good-news headlines are retrieved from publishers' public RSS feeds through rss2json.com, which receives your IP address like any web request.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme, cookie choice and tool entries. Clear your browser data to delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25 or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to processing. Contact us through our contact form (choose "Privacy request").

Children

Positive.Energy is intended for adults. Contests are open only to people aged 18+. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA).

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose they were sent, and use reasonable safeguards to protect them. The site is served over HTTPS.

Changes

We'll post updates on this page with a new date.
