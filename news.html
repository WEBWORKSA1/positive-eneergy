Why read good news?

News coverage leans negative. Bad news grabs attention, so it's what gets produced. Over time a steady diet of it can skew how safe and hopeful the world seems. Constructive journalism doesn't hide problems. It reports on responses to problems: what's working, where and why.

We gather headlines from outlets that specialise in that approach, including Positive News, Good News Network, Greater Good Magazine and Reasons to be Cheerful. We link straight to the original article and show only the headline and a short teaser, so the publishers keep their readers.
