How to Choose a Life Coach: Costs, Credentials and Red Flags

What life coaching is (and isn't), typical costs, credentials to look for, questions to ask and red flags, plus how to get matched for free.

A good coach can help you get unstuck faster than you would alone. But coaching is largely unregulated, so choosing well matters. Here's what to know.

Coaching vs. therapy

Coaching is forward-focused. It helps mentally well people clarify goals, build habits and take action. Therapy is provided by licensed professionals and treats mental health conditions, trauma and emotional distress. If you're struggling with depression, anxiety or trauma, start with a therapist or doctor.

What it costs

Prices vary widely by experience and niche. Typical individual life-coaching sessions often fall in the range of about $75–$250, executive and specialist coaching can be much higher, and group programs are usually cheaper per person. Many coaches sell monthly packages. Most offer a free intro call.

Credentials to look for

  • ICF credentials (ACC, PCC, MCC) from the International Coaching Federation require training hours, client coaching hours and ethics commitments.
  • EMCC accreditation is common in Europe.
  • Relevant experience in your area, for example a career coach who has hired people.

Credentials aren't everything, but they signal training and an ethics code.

Questions to ask on the intro call

  • "What's your coaching approach, and who do you work best with?"
  • "How do you measure progress?"
  • "What does a typical session and the time between sessions look like?"
  • "What happens if it's not a fit?"
  • "When would you refer someone to a therapist?"

Red flags

  • Guaranteed results or income claims.
  • High-pressure sales, "only 2 spots left," or pushing large upfront packages on the first call.
  • Diagnosing or "treating" mental health conditions without a licence.
  • No clear agreement on fees, confidentiality and cancellation.
  • You feel judged rather than challenged.

Fit matters most

Research on helping relationships consistently highlights the quality of the relationship, sometimes called the working alliance, as important to outcomes. Talk to two or three coaches before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a life coach cost?

Many life coaches charge roughly $75–$250 per session, with executive and specialist coaches charging more and group programs less. Most offer a free intro call.

What qualifications should a life coach have?

Look for recognised training such as ICF (ACC, PCC, MCC) or EMCC accreditation, relevant experience and a clear ethics and confidentiality policy.
