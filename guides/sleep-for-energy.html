Sleep for Energy: 10 Ways to Wake Up Refreshed

Practical, evidence-informed sleep habits to wake up with more energy, from light and timing to the bedroom environment.

By the Positive.Energy editorial team · 7 min read · Reviewed November 2026

In this guide
  1. 10 habits for refreshing sleep
  2. When to get help
  3. FAQ

No energy hack beats a good night's sleep. Most adults need around seven to nine hours, according to sleep medicine organisations. Quality and timing matter as much as hours.

10 habits for refreshing sleep

  1. Keep a consistent schedule. Same wake time daily, weekends included.
  2. Get morning daylight. It helps set the timing of evening sleepiness.
  3. Dim the evening. Lower lights and screen brightness in the last hour before bed.
  4. Mind caffeine timing. Keep caffeine to the morning if you're sensitive.
  5. Go easy on alcohol. It may help you fall asleep but tends to disrupt sleep later in the night.
  6. Cool, dark, quiet. A cool room, blackout curtains or an eye mask, and earplugs or white noise.
  7. Wind-down ritual. 20–30 minutes of reading, stretching or a body scan. Try the 4-7-8 breathing pattern.
  8. Park your worries. Write tomorrow's to-do list before bed so your brain can let go.
  9. Bed is for sleep. If you can't sleep after about 20 minutes, get up and do something calm until sleepy.
  10. Be careful with naps. Keep them short and before mid-afternoon.

When to get help

Loud snoring, gasping, persistent insomnia or daytime sleepiness despite enough hours can point to treatable sleep disorders. Speak to a doctor. CBT for insomnia (CBT-I) is a recommended first-line treatment for chronic insomnia.

Frequently asked questions

How can I wake up with more energy?

Keep a consistent wake time, get bright light soon after waking, avoid late caffeine and alcohol, and keep your bedroom cool and dark.

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Most adults need roughly 7–9 hours per night, though individual needs vary.
