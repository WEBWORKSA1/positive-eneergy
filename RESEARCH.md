## Key takeaways applied
1. **Quiz-first funnels convert** (VIA, Lifehack, Greater Good). The Energy Score sits in the hero and every result ends with a coach CTA prefilled with the weakest area.
2. **Daily-return mechanics** (Action for Happiness, Daily Stoic, GNN) mean the Today page and rotation repeat across home, quotes and 404.
3. **Coach marketplaces cap matches** (Noomii "5 coaches") → we cap at 3 for quality and higher per-lead value.
4. **B2B proof and a demo form** (BetterUp) → workplace page with programs, measurement and proposal form.
5. **Trust signals** (Verywell, Psych Central) → editorial standards, hedged claims, help page, FAQ schema: critical for wellbeing content and AdSense approval.
6. **Consent-first forms** (Positive News) → explicit sharing consent in the lead funnel.

## Market data used
- Life coaching typically runs ~$75–$250/session, with monthly packages of ~$500–$1,500 (2026 pricing surveys, e.g. ThePricer and Noomii). MentorCruise lists a median life-coach plan of $240/month.
- Pay-per-lead in local services commonly runs $25–$80+, and coaching clients worth thousands justify higher CPLs. That supports a pay-per-match coach model.
