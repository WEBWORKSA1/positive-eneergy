How to actually use a quote

A quote won't change your life by itself, but used deliberately it becomes a cue. Behaviour researchers call this a "prompt": a small trigger that reminds you of a value at the moment you need it. Three ways to make one stick:

  • Pick one per week and make it your phone lock screen.
  • Pair it with a habit. Read it while the kettle boils, then do the matching practice from Today.
  • Share it with a person, not just a feed. A personal message adds connection, which is the strongest wellbeing booster we know.
