Questions, answered

Is Positive.Energy free?

Yes. Every tool, guide, quote and daily practice is free with no sign-up. We are funded by clearly labelled advertising, optional reader support, and fees from vetted coaches and workplace partners — never by charging you.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. Positive.Energy offers general wellbeing education and self-help tools based on published research. It does not replace a doctor, therapist or crisis service. If you are struggling, please see our Need help now page.

How does coach matching work?

You answer a few questions about your goal, format and budget. With your consent, up to three independent coaches who fit your request contact you to offer a free intro call. You decide whether to continue.

Where is my journal and mood data stored?

Only in your own browser. Your gratitude entries, mood logs and challenge progress never leave your device unless you export them.

How often is the site updated?

Every day. The quote, affirmation, practice, journal prompt and kindness idea rotate daily, and our good-news feed refreshes automatically each morning.
