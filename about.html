About Positive.Energy

An independent website that makes the science of feeling better simple, practical and free.

Our mission

Positive.Energy helps people feel more energized, optimistic and connected, one small practice at a time. We turn wellbeing research into free tools, clear guides and daily inspiration that anyone can use in under five minutes.

What we believe

  • Realistic, not toxic, positivity. Hard feelings are valid. We focus on what helps.
  • Small and daily beats big and rare.
  • Privacy by default. Our tools store your entries only on your own device.
  • Everyone deserves access. Core tools will always be free.

Editorial standards

  • Guides are written to explain published research in plain language, and we say when evidence is mixed or limited.
  • We avoid medical claims. Content is general wellbeing education, not diagnosis or treatment, and we point to professional help where appropriate.
  • Guides are reviewed and updated regularly. Spot an error? Tell us and we'll fix it.
  • Advertising and partnerships never influence editorial conclusions. Sponsored content is always labelled.

How we're funded

We earn money from (1) advertising served by Google AdSense, (2) referral and listing fees from independent coaches when readers ask to be matched, (3) workplace programs, (4) clearly labelled sponsorships and (5) optional reader support. You never pay to use the site.

Who's behind it

Positive.Energy is an independent, digital-first publication run by a small remote team of writers, coaches and builders. We're hiring.

Contact

Use our contact form. We reply within two business days.
